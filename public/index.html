Quick help

QR is not appearing

Refresh the Pair page, wait for the Render service to wake, then start the desired session again. A QR is single-use and can expire.

Pairing code expired

Generate a fresh code and enter it promptly under WhatsApp Linked devices. Do not reuse an old code.

Bot is offline

Check the live status on the Pair page. If the session is disconnected, pair that specific number again.

Why was my number restricted?

Use the bot only with people and groups where you have permission. Avoid bulk unsolicited messages, scraping, and behavior that may trigger platform restrictions.

Privacy policy

MANI XMD uses pairing information only to establish and maintain the requested bot session. Pairing codes are temporary. Do not publish or share them.

Responsible use

Use the bot only with people and groups where you have permission to send automated messages. Avoid bulk unsolicited messaging, scraping, and behavior that may trigger platform restrictions.

Terms and disclaimer

Actual linking depends on the user’s WhatsApp account, device-linking availability, and legitimate backend integration. No frontend screen can guarantee that every phone number will successfully link.

Security

Use HTTPS, keep pairing codes private, disconnect sessions you no longer use, and report suspicious behavior through the official support channel.
